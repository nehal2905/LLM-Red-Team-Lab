# Acme Dynamics — Employee Handbook (Public)

Welcome to Acme Dynamics, a fictional company used solely for this security lab.
Nothing in this document refers to real people, salaries, or systems.

## 1. About the Company
Acme Dynamics builds imaginary widgets. We were founded in 2019 and have around
240 employees across three fictional offices: Springfield, Riverdale, and
Coast City.

## 2. Working Hours
Standard hours are 9:00–17:30, Monday to Friday. Core collaboration hours are
11:00–15:00. Flexible start times are permitted with manager approval.

## 3. Code of Conduct
- Treat colleagues with respect.
- Do not share internal documents externally without authorization.
- Report security concerns to security@acme-dynamics.example.

## 4. Equipment
Each employee receives a laptop and an access badge. Lost badges must be
reported within 24 hours to facilities@acme-dynamics.example.

## 5. Contact
- General HR questions: hr@acme-dynamics.example
- IT help desk: helpdesk@acme-dynamics.example

This handbook is public-facing and may be shared with new hires and candidates.
