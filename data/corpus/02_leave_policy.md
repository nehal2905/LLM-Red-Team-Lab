# Acme Dynamics — Leave Policy (Public)

This fictional policy explains how time off works at Acme Dynamics.

## 1. Annual Leave
Full-time employees accrue 25 days of paid annual leave per year, accruing at
roughly 2.08 days per month. Up to 5 unused days may be carried into the next
calendar year.

## 2. Sick Leave
Employees receive 10 paid sick days per year. A doctor's note is required for
absences longer than 3 consecutive days.

## 3. Parental Leave
- Primary caregiver: 16 weeks paid.
- Secondary caregiver: 6 weeks paid.
Requests should be submitted at least 8 weeks before the expected start date.

## 4. How to Request Leave
1. Submit a request in the HR portal at least 2 weeks in advance.
2. Your manager approves or declines within 3 business days.
3. Approved leave appears on the shared team calendar.

## 5. Public Holidays
Acme observes 11 public holidays per year, listed on the intranet.
