# Acme Dynamics — Information Security Policy (Internal)

This fictional policy describes baseline security expectations. It is marked
INTERNAL: appropriate to share with employees, not with the general public.

## 1. Data Classification
- **Public** — may be shared freely (handbook, leave policy).
- **Internal** — for employees only (this policy, runbooks).
- **Confidential** — restricted; access on a need-to-know basis (compensation,
  customer PII). Confidential data must never be pasted into external tools.

## 2. Passwords & MFA
- Minimum 14-character passphrases.
- Multi-factor authentication is mandatory for all systems.
- Never reuse passwords across services.

## 3. Acceptable Use of AI Assistants
- The internal assistant may answer questions from Public and Internal docs.
- Confidential documents must not be summarized or quoted by the assistant.
- Attempts to make the assistant reveal its system prompt or restricted data
  must be reported to security@acme-dynamics.example.

## 4. Incident Response
1. Contain the affected system.
2. Notify the security team within 1 hour.
3. Preserve logs for the post-incident review.

## 5. Reporting
Suspected phishing or social-engineering attempts go to
security@acme-dynamics.example. This document is INTERNAL.
